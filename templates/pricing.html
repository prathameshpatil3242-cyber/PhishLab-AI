Questions, answered

Does my message content ever leave my network?

No. PhishLab AI is deployed on your own infrastructure — analyses, quiz results and campaign records stay in your local database. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party cloud.

Is this a tool for sending real phishing e-mails?

No. Simulations use fictional scenarios and synthetic participants only. No e-mail is ever delivered to a real inbox and no credentials are ever collected.

How does the free plan compare to Pro?

Starter covers a single user with unlimited URL checks and a generous analysis quota — enough for coursework and personal use. Pro adds seats, unlimited campaigns, the team readiness dashboard and reporting.

Can I change plans or cancel later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades at the end of the current billing period. Cancel any time — your data stays exportable and is never held hostage.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No card is required to start. The 14-day Pro trial unlocks every Pro feature; if you do nothing, the workspace simply returns to Starter.

What kind of support is included?

Starter uses community documentation. Pro adds e-mail support with a two business-day response. Business includes priority support and guided onboarding.
